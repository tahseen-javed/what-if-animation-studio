What if every light in your city went out, all at once, for one whole night?

Nine p.m. The fridge stops humming. The street goes silent. Phones still glow, so everyone looks down, waiting.

Ten p.m. Batteries start to die. Neighbours step outside, because inside is darker than out.

Eleven. Someone looks up. Then everyone does.

Above a city, light washes the sky away. Normally you see a few dozen stars. In a truly dark sky, your eyes can pick out about two thousand five hundred.

Midnight. The Milky Way shows up for the first time in years. Kids who have never seen it ask what it is.

Then, at five in the morning, the power comes back. The fridge hums. The stars fade.

So here is the question. If you could choose, would you switch it back on?
